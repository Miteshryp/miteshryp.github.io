## Research and Interests
I am currently working with Prof. [V. Krishna Nandivada](https://www.cse.iitm.ac.in/~krishna/) in the field of Compiler Optimizations for ML systems. Our work involves inference optimizations for accelerator architectures. 

Along with my work, I am also deeply interested in learning about all layers of system software stack, including Deep Learning model training and inferencing techiques, infrastructure supporting tools, underlying hardwares, compilers, as well as hardware-software co-design paradigms.

I am also fond of having spirited discussions about formal methods and their applications in critical real-world infrastructures with my lab collegues, but since I am a newbie in this space - I'd like to learn more about this  :))!

If you find yourself aligned to any of these topics, or if you feel you may benefit from a lively discussion on them, please feel free to get in touch with me over mail or linkedin!
